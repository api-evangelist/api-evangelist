I keep coming back to the same wall. Every company is “all in on AI” right now, every roadmap has an agent on it, every keynote promises autonomous software that goes off and does the work for you. And then you go to actually wire one of these agents up to their API and the first thing they ask you to do is open a browser, log in as a human, click around a dashboard, and prove you are a person.
