One of the things Z and I talked about in our recent conversation (that is Zdenek Nemec, of Apiary and Superface) was his engagement with younger engineers and entrepreneurs about how agents will onboard with APIs. The idea is that this new generation of engineers will see things differently and approach all of this without the baggage the rest of us carry. They won’t see the need for portals, documentation, SDKs, and all the other elements we’ve been bringing to the table so that humans and agents can onboard with and use APIs.
