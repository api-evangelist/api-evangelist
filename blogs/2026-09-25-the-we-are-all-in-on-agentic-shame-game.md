In my recent conversation with Zdenek Nemec, Z of Apiary and Superface fame, we talked about just how hard it is for agents to onboard with your APIs. I am painfully aware of this in my own work, but Z brings even deeper experience, and pain, from being on the front lines of agentic onboarding. He dove head first into solving this problem just as the AI shift was beginning, around the time I was contemplating leaving Postman.
