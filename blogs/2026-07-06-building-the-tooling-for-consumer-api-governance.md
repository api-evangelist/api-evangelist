A couple of weeks ago I wrote about consumer API governance in an agentic world, and I keep making the argument from stage — APIDays New York, Amsterdam, and Munich next month. Arguments only go so far, though. A decade of vendor messaging has trained everyone to believe governance is something you do at design time while you produce an API, and you do not unwind that belief with a talk.
