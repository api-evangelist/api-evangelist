Agent skills are the newest thing I am watching closely, and they are the clearest case yet of a technology that brings real, obvious value and a fresh set of problems in the exact same package. A skill is packaged procedural knowledge — a bundle of instructions, and often some scripts and resources, that teaches an agent how to do a specific job well, portable from one context to another.
