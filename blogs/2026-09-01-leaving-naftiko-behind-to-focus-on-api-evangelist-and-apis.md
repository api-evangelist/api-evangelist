You might have noticed the shift in my focus over the summer, but I want to be explicit about my departure from Naftiko for anyone who was away doing summer things, or who missed the signal in all the noise. After a year at Naftiko, I am leaving behind the startup I helped found to focus exclusively on API discovery with APIs.io and API governance with API Evangelist. The company and the product were not aligned with my vision for the API space, and as with all of my recent work, my vision is what propels me forward, not the paycheck or the venture capital investment.
