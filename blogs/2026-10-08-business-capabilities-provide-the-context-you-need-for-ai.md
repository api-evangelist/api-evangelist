I have been talking about capabilities for about two years now as some mystical, far-off destination I was headed toward from the landing of API engineering. A focus on API products was one of the reasons I left Postman for Bloomberg, and since leaving Bloomberg I have been searching for meaningful narratives that would resonate with business stakeholders and leadership. API products resonate well with the middle run of stakeholders in newer small and medium-sized organizations, but I am still on a quest for what will speak to leadership and larger enterprises.
