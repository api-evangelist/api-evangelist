I keep coming back to the same wall. Every company tells me they are all in on AI, that agents are the future, that machines will be first-class consumers of their software. And then to get a set of credentials I have to log into a dashboard, click through a wizard, name my application by hand, and copy a secret out of a web form into a clipboard.
