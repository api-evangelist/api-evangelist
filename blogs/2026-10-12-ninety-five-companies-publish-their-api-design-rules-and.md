There is a new Rules section on APIs.io, and it exists to answer one question I have been asked for years and could never answer with data: who actually publishes the rules their APIs are designed to? Not who has a style guide in a wiki, and not who lints in CI behind the firewall. Who writes the rules down as a Spectral ruleset, serves it where anyone can read it, and puts their name on it.
