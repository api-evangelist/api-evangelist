I keep coming back to the same contradiction. Every company I talk to is “all in on AI,” wiring up agents that are supposed to act on their behalf across dozens of services, and yet the front door to most of those services is still a web form, a human-in-the-loop approval, and a captcha to prove you are not the very thing they just told their board they are betting the company on. Onboarding does not scale.
