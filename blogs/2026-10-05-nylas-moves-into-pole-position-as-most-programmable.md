There is a new name at the top of APIs.io. Of the 29,074 API providers in the catalog, ranked by the Kin Score, Nylas is now first, at 93.2 out of 100. That puts it in the exemplar band, up 16.5 points from where it sat for most of September, and agent-native on agent readiness at 57.
