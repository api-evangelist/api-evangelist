I spent the summer studying the impact AI is having on higher education with my intern Emily Barton. We each dug into how AI is being used on the campuses of universities relevant to our own worlds — Emily looked at her school and the wider California system, and I looked at NYU and Columbia. July produced two essays from each of us, and this represents the second essay from me.
