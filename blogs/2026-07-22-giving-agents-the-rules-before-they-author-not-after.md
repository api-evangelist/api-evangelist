I have been walking through the governance tools I keep building under API Commons, one a day, and today’s is the one that finally admits something I have been circling for a while: a lot of the APIs being written right now are not being written by people. They are being generated by agents, and the way we govern generated work is still to let the machine produce something and then lint it afterward and tell it everything it got wrong. That is backwards.
