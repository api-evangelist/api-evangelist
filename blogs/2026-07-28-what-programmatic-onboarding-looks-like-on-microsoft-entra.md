I keep coming back to the same frustration, and I am going to keep writing about it until enough people are annoyed with me to do something. Onboarding to an API does not scale. You can have the cleanest OpenAPI on the planet, a beautiful docs portal, and a pricing page that actually makes sense, and none of it matters if a developer or an agent still has to sit in a browser, click through a settings panel, and hand-copy a client ID and secret into a config file.
