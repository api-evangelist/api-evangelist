I keep coming back to the same wall. Every time I want to get a piece of software talking to an API, I find out that the part everyone treats as a formality, getting credentials, is the part that doesn’t scale. You can wire up an agent to reason, plan, and call tools all day long, but somewhere in the middle of that workflow there is a human being copying a client secret out of a web console and pasting it into a config file.
