I keep coming back to the same wall. Onboarding to an API does not scale. We have built a generation of tools that are supposed to act on our behalf, that are supposed to wire themselves up to the services we already pay for, and yet the very first step still assumes a human with a mouse, a browser tab, and a few minutes to click around a dashboard.
