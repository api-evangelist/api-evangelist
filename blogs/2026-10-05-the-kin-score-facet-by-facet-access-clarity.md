This is part five of nine, one Kin Score facet each business day. On Friday I covered Developer Ergonomics, which is about how easy it is to get started once you are in. Access Clarity asks the question before that one: what does it cost, what am I allowed to do, and how do I get in, without having to book a call with sales?
