Almost all of the noise about MCP has been about tools. Everybody ships tools, everybody argues about tools, everybody counts how many tools their server exposes as if that were the score. But the protocol has two other primitives that get a fraction of the attention and are, I would argue, the genuinely underrated good parts: prompts and resources.
