I have been walking through the governance tools I keep building under API Commons one at a time, and today’s is the one that turns the lens back on your own plumbing. Most of these tools ask whether your API is any good. The Governance Pipeline Auditor asks a different and more uncomfortable question: is the machinery that is supposed to be checking your API actually doing its job?
