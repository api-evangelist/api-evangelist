API Evangelist has spent a long time arguing that the machine-readable contract is the center of gravity for an API. OpenAPI for the request and response, JSON Schema for the shape of the data, AsyncAPI for the events. Every conversation about governance, discovery, testing, SDKs, and now agents runs back through those three specifications.
