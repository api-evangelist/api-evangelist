This essay is by Emily Barton, who spent the summer of 2026 researching how universities are actually adopting AI, alongside Kin Lane, for API Evangelist. After starting in California, her August research widened out to Europe and the UK. It is published as the companion to Kin’s essay, Scoring the Impact AI Is Having on Higher Education, which assesses the same summer from the API and infrastructure side.
