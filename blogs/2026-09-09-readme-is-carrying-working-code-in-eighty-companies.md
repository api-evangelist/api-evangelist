Asana, 8x8, AppsFlyer, Fireblocks, Brandwatch, Ironclad, Demandbase, Bazaarvoice, Feedly, Frontegg, Dotdigital — sixty companies in the union once you count the whole family, which also includes x-readme-fauxas in eighteen providers and x-readme-hidden. Then I looked at what is actually inside the key, and it turned out to be more interesting than I expected.
